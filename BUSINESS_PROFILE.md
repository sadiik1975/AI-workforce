# Been Ventures Inc. Business Profile

## Business

Been Ventures Inc.

## Primary initiative

BoroughBuild

## Target market

HVAC contractors first, with later expansion into plumbers and electricians.

## Services

- website development
- AI customer-facing chat
- lead capture
- local SEO
- Google Business Profile support
- hosting
- website maintenance

## Philosophy

Keep the business simple and efficient. Prefer tools that help the owner operate with less manual work over complex, expensive solutions.

## Zero-budget guiding principles

- use free/open-source tools where practical
- avoid unnecessary paid services
- do not require VS Code or a graphical editor to operate
- keep local development lightweight
- document any service that requires money
